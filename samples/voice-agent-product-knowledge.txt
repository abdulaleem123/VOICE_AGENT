VOICE AGENT PRO — PRODUCT KNOWLEDGE BASE
=========================================

COMPANY
-------
Voice Agent Pro helps B2B teams qualify inbound and outbound conversations, capture leads,
and book meetings with decision-makers (CEO, CFO, CTO, VP Sales, Founders).

PRODUCT OVERVIEW
----------------
- AI voice and chat agent trained on your documents (PDF, DOC, TXT)
- Qualifies visitors by name, company, and job title
- Classifies personas (CEO, CFO, CTO, etc.)
- Hands off pricing and NDA conversations to humans
- Books discovery, demo, and pricing meetings automatically
- Single-tenant: one company, one agent, one knowledge base

KEY FEATURES
------------
1. Agent configuration — name, tone, greeting, target titles, qualification rounds
2. Knowledge base — upload product docs; agent answers from your content only
3. Live conversation — typed chat, microphone (speech-to-text), spoken replies (text-to-speech)
4. Leads pipeline — captures contact details and persona classification
5. Handoff rules — pricing, NDA, meeting requests, human transfer
6. Meeting scheduler — books follow-up calls instead of quoting live on the call
7. Admin console — health checks, OpenAI cost tracking, inbound/outbound call totals

WHO IT IS FOR
-------------
- SaaS companies with inbound demo requests
- Sales teams doing outbound to executives
- Agencies qualifying CFO/CTO/CEO leads before a live call

INTEGRATIONS
------------
- OpenAI API for chat (GPT), embeddings, Whisper speech-to-text, and TTS voices
- Works in the browser today; phone numbers (Twilio) can be added later

-------------

For NDA requests, collect the visitor's name, company, and email, then schedule
an NDA review call with our sales or legal team.

MEETING TYPES
-------------
- Discovery call (30 min) — learn about their use case
- Product demo (45 min) — live walkthrough
- Pricing call (30 min) — custom quote discussion
- NDA review (20 min) — paperwork and next steps

COMMON QUESTIONS
----------------
Q: Does it replace my sales team?
A: No. It qualifies and books meetings; humans close deals.

Q: Can it use our own documents?
A: Yes. Upload PDF, Word, or text files to the knowledge base.

Q: Is it multi-tenant?
A: No. This deployment is single-tenant — one company per instance.

Q: What voices are available?
A: Multiple OpenAI voices including Nova, Shimmer, Echo, Onyx, and others.
